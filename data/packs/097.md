[S6] https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/integrations/what-is-higgsfield-mcp (official)
Higgsfield MCP connects Higgsfield to AI agents. Connect once and generate images, videos, characters, and audio from Claude or any MCP-compatible agent, without leaving your agent. The main difference from generating on the web: MCP always uses credits. Unlimited access and free generations apply only on higgsfield.ai, so anything generated through MCP, CLI, Canvas, Supercomputer, or other automated tools deducts credits at standard rates.
What is Higgsfield MCP?
MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open protocol that lets AI agents connect to external services and call their tools. Higgsfield MCP is a connector that lets you generate images, videos, characters, and audio from Higgsfield directly inside Claude or another MCP-compatible agent. It isn't the Higgsfield API: MCP connects to your existing account and uses your existing plan credits, with no API key required. For building generation into your own product with per-request billing, see What is the Higgsfield API, and how do I get started?
