Paygent is a wallet your AI agents pay from. You add money, connect an agent and give it a daily limit. The agent can ask to pay, and nothing is paid until you approve it with your PIN.
How it works
- Open a wallet at paygenthq.ai, confirm your phone and set your approval PIN.
- Add money by bank transfer to your wallet account number, direct debit or stablecoin.
- Connect an agent with the Paygent connector (steps below). When it connects, you approve it with your PIN and choose its slot: a name, a daily limit and the tools it may use.
- Ask your agent to buy something. At the checkout it prepares the payment and asks you. You see who is paid, how much and why, and approve with your PIN on Paygent’s website or phone app.
- The agent pays and files the receipt. Every payment is kept in Activity under the agent that made it.
Connect an agent
Every agent uses the same connector: name Paygent, address https://paygenthq.ai/mcp. Each agent you approve gets its own slot, up to five.

The Paygent backend exposes a small REST API. The SDK uses it. You also call it directly to manage plans, users, and subscriptions.

Authentication: Bearer token in Authorization: Bearer pg_live_... header. The product creation endpoint (POST /products) is the only exception — it's how you mint the first key.
Content type: application/json for all request and response bodies.

Contact us
Write from the email on your Paygent account, so we can find it. Include the payment or receipt number if your question is about a payment.
Never send us your PINs, your BVN or a full card number. We will never ask for them, by email or anywhere else.
If something is urgent
- An agent is doing something you didn’t ask for: open Agents in your wallet and pause or disconnect it. It loses access at once.
- You think someone knows your PIN: change it in Settings, then email us.
- The agent card was used without your approval: open Card and freeze it, then email us.
Common questions
An approval request expired
No money moved. Ask your agent to prepare the payment again and approve the new request.
A payment shows as failed
The amount returns to your wallet. Check the recipient’s details with your agent and try again, or email us with the payment number.
My deposit hasn’t arrived
Bank transfers usually arrive within minutes. If it has been more than an hour, email us with the amount, the time and your bank.
My agent can’t connect
Check it is using the address https://paygenthq.ai/mcp, and that you approved it with your PIN on the Paygent page it opened. The help page has the steps for each agent.

Over the standards you would pick anyway.
Paygent is not a proprietary API — it is an agent built on the wire models of the Universal Commerce Protocol: a discovery document and an MCP checkout endpoint modelled on UCP, plus an agent card. Conformance to the current UCP release is on the roadmap. If your runtime can call a tool, it can search, recommend how to pay for the most value on each cart, spend points, and complete the checkout.
All money and points math is deterministic and server-side — never delegated to a model.

Standard surfaces
 Integrate the way the ecosystem already works.
No proprietary SDK required to start — you talk to Paygent through a discovery document and
 an MCP checkout modelled on UCP. Conformance to the current UCP release is on the roadmap.
🪪
Agent card at /.well-known/agent_card.json: the commerce agent's identity, capabilities and endpoint. Signing keys are pinned out of band at onboarding, never fetched over the network.
🧭
The /.well-known/ucp document advertises its checkout capability and MCP endpoint, machine-readable.
🔧
Create, get, add items to, complete, and cancel checkouts as MCP tool calls — completion and cancellation are idempotent on the caller's key.
